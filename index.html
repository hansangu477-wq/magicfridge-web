FAQ
Questions?
We got you.
Is Gmail access required?+
Not at all. You can add items manually by scanning a receipt photo. Connecting Gmail just makes everything automatic and hands-free.
Which stores are supported?+
Coupang (Korea), Amazon (US/Global), Rakuten (Japan), Taobao (China). More stores are being added regularly.
Is my email data private?+
Yes. We only scan for receipt keywords — your raw email content is never stored or shared. All data is deleted immediately when you remove your account.
iOS version coming?+
Android only for now. iOS is on the roadmap — follow us to stay updated.
What languages are supported?+
Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese, German, French and Spanish. The app detects your device language automatically.
Can I share the fridge with my family?+
Yes! With the Pro plan you can share your fridge with family members using an invite code. Everyone can see and update the inventory in real time.
