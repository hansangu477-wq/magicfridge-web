FAQ
Questions?
We got you.
Is Gmail access required?+
Not at all. Add items manually without linking Gmail. Connecting it just makes everything hands-free.
Which stores are supported?+
Coupang, Naver, Emart, Amazon, Rakuten, AliExpress, Shopee and more — growing regularly.
Is my email data private?+
Yes. We only scan for receipt keywords. Raw email is never stored. Everything deleted on account removal.
iOS version?+
Android only for now. iOS is on the roadmap.
Is it free?+
Everything is free during beta. Core features stay free after launch.
